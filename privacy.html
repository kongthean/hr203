Privacy Policy

Version 2 · Effective 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Thean team ("we", "us") collects on kongthean.com and in Thean Studio, our publishing app, why we collect it, how we keep it safe, and the choices you have. By using our website or the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Thean is a Cambodian travel team that shares places, culture and travel tips on kongthean.com and on our social media pages. We also run Thean Studio, an app that helps people publish videos and photos to their own Facebook Page, Instagram account and Telegram channel. Contact: info@kongthean.com.

2. Information we collect on the website

  • Chat and calls: your messages, the name you give, and call details (time and length). A call is recorded only if you are told before it starts.
  • Forms and feedback: anything you choose to send us, such as a rating, message, phone number or email.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, a technical address that lets us send notifications to your device.
  • Visitor statistics: we use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular, which country and type of device visitors use, and how they found our website. It uses cookies and does not tell us your name. You can block it with your browser's privacy settings or an ad blocker.
  • Settings on your device: your language and theme, saved in your own browser.

3. Information we collect in Thean Studio

  • Account details: email, name, phone number, Facebook profile link, the date you joined, and your acceptance of the Terms of Use.
  • Activity history: for each post made through the App, the time, the places it was posted, the Page name, the source link and the caption.
  • Plans and payments: the plan you chose, the amount, the receipt picture you upload, the text our system reads from it, the transaction number and the result of the check.
  • Support: your chats with our team and our assistant.
  • Free post count and account status, such as a pause.

4. Information that stays only on your device

Your Facebook, Instagram and Telegram access keys, your caption settings and your recent posts list are saved only in your own browser. We do not receive or store them on our servers. You can remove them in Posting settings, and they are removed when you sign out.

5. How we use information

  • To provide the website and the App, and to publish posts you request.
  • To check payments, turn plans on, and prevent fake or reused receipts.
  • To answer your questions and give support.
  • To keep the service safe, investigate misuse, and enforce our Terms of Use.
  • To meet our legal obligations under the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.
  • To understand and improve our service.

6. When we share information

  • Service providers that run our service: Google Firebase (accounts and database), Cloudinary (photos, videos and receipt pictures), and GitHub (website hosting and automatic checks).
  • Google Analytics: visitor statistics for the website, as explained in section 2.
  • Platforms you choose: when you post, your Content is sent to Facebook, Instagram or Telegram and is then covered by their own privacy policies.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it, or when we find a serious violation of our Terms of Use, we may share account information and activity history with the competent authorities of the Kingdom of Cambodia.
  • We never sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

7. How long we keep information

  • Website chats and messages: as long as needed to help you, and up to 12 months.
  • App account details: while your account is open.
  • Activity history, payments, receipts and support chats: while your account is open and up to 2 years after it is closed, to handle disputes, fraud and legal requests.
  • Longer, if the law or an ongoing investigation requires it.

8. How we protect information

Information is stored with trusted providers that use encryption in transit. Only our team can see customer records. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase and are never visible to us. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

9. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your details in My account at any time.
  • Delete your account in My account, or ask us to delete your information by email or chat. We reply within 30 days. Some records may be kept as explained in section 7.
  • Turn notifications off at any time in your browser or on the website.
  • Remove your social media keys from your device in Posting settings.

10. Children

Thean Studio is not for people under 18 without the permission of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child gave us information without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. When we make an important change, we will tell you in the App.
